FIELD GUIDANCE / PUBLIC

Small signals can
precede a slide.

Leave early when conditions change. This prototype complements — never replaces — official advisories and local disaster-management instructions.

01

Fresh cracks or tilting trees

Move away from the slope, do not cross cracks, and report the location.

02

Unusual sounds or water seepage

Leave low-lying areas and follow the instructions of local authorities.

03

Debris across a road

Do not attempt to drive through it. Share a photo to help responders act.
